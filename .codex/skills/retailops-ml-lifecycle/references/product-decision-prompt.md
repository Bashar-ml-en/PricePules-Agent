# Product decision

For every proposal, answer:

1. Why: named buyer, painful recurring workflow, and why it is worth testing.
2. What: smallest useful capability and explicit non-goals.
3. How: authorised inputs, deterministic or agent roles, lifecycle gates, and
   human decision.
4. Impact: pilot baseline, measurable outcomes, time window, and unknowns.

Return PROCEED, REFINE, or REJECT with the next smallest experiment. Do not
present assumptions, benchmark results, or synthetic data as production
evidence.
